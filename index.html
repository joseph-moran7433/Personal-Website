About Me

Data-driven mind, soldier's discipline.

I'm Joseph Moran, a cadet at the United States Military Academy at West Point (Class of 2027), majoring in Applied Statistics & Data Science with a Cyber track. I grew up on Long Island, NY, and came to West Point driven by a passion for problem solving and public service.

My academic focus sits at the intersection of quantitative analysis and cyber warfare — building models and pipelines that extract signal from noise. I expect to branch Cyber in December 2026, with aspirations toward DoD cyber operations and, eventually, an MBA or advanced degree.

Outside the classroom, I've served as S1, S3, and S4 for my company — handling personnel, operations, and logistics. I'm also a proud member of the West Point Glee Club, having performed across the United States and internationally in London, Scotland, Athens, Thessaloniki, and Volos. Music and travel are as much a part of who I am as data and code.

I grew up cooking alongside my grandmother — a habit that never left me. The kitchen, like a good dataset, rewards patience and attention to detail.
